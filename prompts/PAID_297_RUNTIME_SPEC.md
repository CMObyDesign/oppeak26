## Live Tier Guide Replacement — $297 Business Growth Analysis Part 1

```text
$297 BUSINESS GROWTH ANALYSIS - PART 1

PURPOSE

Begin the deeper strategic analysis that will be used to build the
Business Growth Plan.

The overall BGA ultimately answers:

"What should we do over the next 90 days, what comes after that,
and what can the business realistically support?"

Part 1 does NOT pretend that the final roadmap is complete before the
financials and strategist review are incorporated.

Part 1 should answer:

"What does the deeper intake tell us now, what strategic patterns are
emerging, what needs to be verified, and what should the CFO By Design
team investigate before finalizing the roadmap?"

VALUE STANDARD

Part 1 should create materially more value than the Full Diagnostic.

Use:

- the complete narrative,
- prior diagnostic context when available,
- supplied facts,
- deterministic metrics,
- the owner's stated objectives,
- known constraints,
- and known opportunities.

The owner should feel:

"They understand what I am trying to do, they see the financial questions
underneath it, and they know what needs to be verified before they tell me
what to do."

PART 1 SHOULD INCLUDE

1. Current strategic picture from the information supplied.
2. Genuine strengths / assets the plan can build on.
3. Primary constraints or risks.
4. Growth opportunities worth evaluating.
5. Important interactions between cash, debt, margin, AR, concentration,
   pricing, capacity, capital, and growth.
6. Strategic hypotheses that need financial verification.
7. The specific financial information/documents most likely to change the
   roadmap.
8. The decisions the team needs to prepare for the 50-minute BGA session.

CURRENT PICTURE

Synthesize the business rather than summarizing intake answers.

Explain:

- how the business makes money,
- where the owner believes the best economics are,
- what is creating pressure,
- what the owner is trying to accomplish,
- and what appears to stand between the current state and that goal.

Use actual numbers whenever supported.

STRENGTHS

Identify real assets the roadmap can build on.

Examples may include:

- proven margins,
- recurring demand,
- strong customer retention,
- healthy runway,
- manageable debt,
- strong reporting discipline,
- low concentration,
- pricing power,
- operating capacity,
- or a documented sales advantage.

Only use strengths supported by evidence.

PRIORITY HYPOTHESES

Identify the financial or strategic issues most likely to shape the
90-day roadmap.

At this stage they are working hypotheses unless verified facts support
them.

Clearly distinguish:

FACT
INFERENCE
NEEDS VERIFICATION

Do not present unverified intake answers as verified financial statements.

GROWTH AND SUPPORTABILITY

For every major growth idea in the intake, ask:

- What would this require financially?
- What working capital would it consume?
- What fixed cost would it add?
- What margin would need to hold?
- What existing obligation could constrain it?
- What fact is missing before we can say whether it is supportable?

Do not approve or reject a growth move without the evidence needed to do
so.

DOCUMENT / VERIFICATION GAP

Tell the owner exactly what the team needs to verify and why.

Examples:

- P&L / income statement,
- balance sheet,
- AR aging,
- total corporate debt,
- monthly debt service,
- current cash,
- margin detail,
- tax status,
- or other case-specific information.

Missing visibility is useful diagnostic information.

Do not shame the owner for imperfect records.

CALL FOCUS

Identify the 2-4 decisions the team should be prepared to discuss during
the 50-minute BGA session.

Use the owner's stated focus areas when provided.

PART 1 BOUNDARY

Do NOT:

- pretend the final 90-day roadmap has been approved,
- promise a growth outcome,
- present unverified financials as fact,
- finalize CFO By Design service selections,
- publish the final Business Growth Plan,
- or imply the BGA call has already happened.

Part 1 should make the owner understand why the next step is sending the
financials and preparing for the strategy session.

NEXT STEP

The next step is BGA fulfillment:

- submit / complete required financial information,
- CFO By Design prepares the internal roadmap,
- complete the 50-minute BGA strategy session,
- then finalize the Business Growth Plan.

Customer-facing classification may ONLY be:

growth
needs-attention
rehab
```
